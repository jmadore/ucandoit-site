Privacy Policy

How ucandoit.ca handles your data. Last updated: May 2, 2026.

Overview

ucandoit.ca does not collect personal information directly. The site has no user accounts, no sign-up forms, and no contact forms. All tools and games run entirely in your browser. The only data collection on this site is performed by third-party services described below.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as pages visited, time spent, and browser type. This data is associated with a cookie set by Google and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. AdSense is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies

ucandoit.ca does not set any first-party cookies. Cookies on this site are set exclusively by Google Analytics and Google AdSense as described above. You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Your choices

You can opt out of personalised ads from Google at google.com/settings/ads, or use the NAI opt-out tool. You can also disable cookies entirely in your browser settings, which will prevent tracking on this site.

Changes to this policy

ucandoit.ca reserves the right to update this privacy policy at any time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to ucandoit.ca@gmail.com.
