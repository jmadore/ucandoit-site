About ucandoit.ca

Canadian tools, games, and timelines — free for everyone.

What we are

ucandoit.ca is a free collection of Canadian-themed word tools, calculators, browser games, and interactive history timelines. Everything is built for curious Canadians — whether you're practising with a word unscrambler, planning a mortgage, playing a numbers game, or exploring Canadian history.

No accounts needed

Every tool and game on this site runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is stored on our servers, and no account or sign-up is ever required.
