How to Master the Numbers Round

Pick six tiles from the small and large stacks, then press the target button to start the 30-second round. Auto pick uses the familiar one-large, five-small mix.

Your expression may use any selected number once or not at all. Exact target hits score 10 points, and the round reveals a possible solution afterward.
