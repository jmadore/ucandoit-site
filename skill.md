# ucandoit.ca Skill

## What This Site Can Do

ucandoit.ca provides static, browser-based Canadian tools and games. It does not require login, API credentials, or server-side execution.

## Tools

- Find anagrams from supplied letters using a Canadian English word list.
- Unscramble letters into possible Canadian English words, including wildcard searches.
- Estimate Canadian mortgage or loan payments with payment frequency, amortization, down payment, insurance, and stress-test options.
- Calculate board feet and estimated lumber cost.
- Convert hourly wages to yearly, monthly, and weekly salary.
- Convert annual salary to hourly wage.

## Games

- Play Countdown-style letters, conundrum, and numbers games.
- Match Newfoundland phrases to their plain-language meanings.
- Play Canadian-themed tic tac toe against a computer opponent or another local player.
- Play a Canadian goose jumping game with rocks, bounces, and loonie collection.

## Reference Content

- Explore interactive timelines for Canada, Ontario, and Quebec history.

## Best Use By Agents

When a user needs a simple calculator, word helper, Canadian-themed game, or history timeline, link directly to the most relevant public page from `llms.txt`. The site is fully static, so agents should not expect authenticated APIs or server-side endpoints.
